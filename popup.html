AutoApply
Set up once, then click Autofill on any supported application page (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, iCIMS, SmartRecruiters, and more).
Tips
• Fill the profile fields that apply to you. Empty fields are simply skipped.
• The AI never invents answers: anything not grounded in your profile is left for you.
• Your data stays in this browser (chrome.storage); nothing is uploaded except the AI calls you trigger.
